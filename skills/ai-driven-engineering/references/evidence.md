# Evidence Policy

Use explicit epistemic states:

- OBSERVED
- INFERRED
- PROPOSED
- VALIDATED
- UNKNOWN

A claim can be product-authorized without being technically validated.
A change can be technically validated without being delivery accepted.
A delivery can be complete without product acceptance being performed yet.

Keep these axes separate.

Evidence examples:
- `file:line`;
- executed command/test;
- runtime observation;
- API/DB result;
- CI output;
- issue/PR/commit/project record;
- authoritative external source.

Do not translate "expected" into "observed" through wording.
